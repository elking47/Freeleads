How it works

While you sleep, a four-stage engine prospects for you.

Every night between 10 PM and 8 AM, Freeleads runs the same pipeline. You only see what survives it.

  1. 10 PM

    Scraping

    We crawl LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and Welcome to the Jungle, then dedupe and store 5,000 to 15,000 new listings.

  2. 3 AM

    AI filtering

    A language model drops permanent roles and internships, tags the discipline and scores freelance fit and urgency. About 80% of listings are cut.

  3. 5 AM

    Enrichment

    We identify who actually buys (the CTO for a web gig, the CMO for marketing) and find their business email, phone and LinkedIn.

  4. 8 AM

    Delivery

    Your dashboard fills with the day's Freeleads, prioritized by your disciplines. An email tells you they're ready.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do the opportunities come from?

From public job listings on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and Welcome to the Jungle. Many companies post a full-time role when what they really need is a few weeks of expert work. Freeleads finds those listings and turns them into leads.

What is a "hidden" freelance need?

A listing that looks like a job but describes a mission: a fixed duration, a launch date, a one-off project, a part-time workload. Our model scores each listing for freelance fit and tells you why, in plain words.

How do you find the decision-maker's contact details?

We work out who buys this kind of work (for example the CTO for a web project) and query professional enrichment providers for their business email, phone and LinkedIn profile. We only handle professional contact data, and we remove anyone who asks us to.

Do you take a commission on missions I sign?

Never. You pay a flat monthly subscription and you keep 100% of what you bill.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick Solo, Pro or Team to keep receiving Freeleads. Your pipeline, notes and templates stay where they are.

Can I change my disciplines later?

Yes, at any time in Settings. Solo includes 1 discipline, Pro 3 and Team all 8.
